===========================
Claim Timesheets
===========================

Each employee can encode and track their time spent on the different claims.


Key Features
============
- Link claims with **Projects** and **Tasks**.
- Record timesheets directly from the claim.
- View planned, spent, and remaining hours in forms, lists, and the portal.
- Statistics button in tasks to access related claims.

Configuration
=============
1. Go to **CRM → Claims** and open or create a claim.
2. Assign a **Project** and optionally a **Task**.
3. Record timesheets; hours are automatically calculated.
4. From **Project Tasks**, use the **Claims** button to navigate to related claims.
5. In the **Timesheet Portal**, related claims will be displayed.
